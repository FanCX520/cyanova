What is Cyanova?+

Cyanova is a company exploring how to create fairer access to opportunity, resources and support for doers, makers and dreamers, including individuals and businesses.

What can I use today?+

We’re testing possible paths with an invitation-only circle. A public set of services has not been announced. This website introduces our direction and provides a way to express interest.

Why invitation-only?+

We’re early. Working with a smaller circle allows us to explore thoughtfully and learn before opening more broadly.

What happens after I introduce myself?+

When the invitation form is connected, introductions will help us understand your interests and consider fit for future testing. An introduction does not guarantee an invitation or a response date.
